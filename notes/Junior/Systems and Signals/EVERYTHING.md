
Discrete Time Fourier Transform
$$
\boxed{
\begin{align}
X(e^{j\omega}) & = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} x[n]e^{-j \hat{\omega}n}\\ 
x[n]  & = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{\hat{\omega}=2\pi}^{} X(e^{j \hat{\omega}})e^{j \hat{\omega}n}d \hat{\omega} \\
\end{align}
}
$$


Discrete Time Fourier Series

$$
\boxed{
\begin{align} 
a_{k}  & = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{n=\left< N \right>  }^{} x[n]e^{-jk \hat{\omega_{0}}n}\\
x[n]  & = \sum_{k=\left< N \right>  }^{} a_{k} e^{jk \hat{\omega_{0}}n} \\

\end{align}
}
$$
