- Why did the researchers first test the polypeptide materials in lithium-metal half-cells before assembling a fully metal-free polypeptide battery? What information does this provide about the materials?

They wanted to see how each individually performed so that they could understand the tricky behavior of the full cell - since that looks like a combination of both individual cells.
