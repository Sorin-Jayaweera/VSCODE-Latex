Regular perovskites have variable orientations between each unit cell, which allows for deformation and many phonon modes. By adding a rigid backbone of PMMA-Graphite-PMMA, the perovskite crystals are far more rigid (massive young's modulus). This reduces lattice deformation from long exposure to sunlight and from high tilt angle, and decreases the number of cracks that can form / how they spread. The material still does melt a little under sun, but far less than pure perovskites.
