- Explain what happens in a typical LIB cycle, and identify the primary mechanism behind battery degradation
Lithium ions have side reactions during recharge cycling and are no longer available as charge carriers, thus reducing the total capacity of the battery over use.

- What does this work do to potentially improve battery lifetime?
They inject new lithium ions into deficient batteries to replenish the supply. This is done by dissolving LiSO2CF3 into the electrolytes, and then off gassing the SO2 and C2F3 or CHF which form after depositing the lithium into the battery structure. 

- How do you think this technology could be implemented in the future or how would you suggest improving this technology?

For this to be useful, they have to collect spent batteries, reinject them, and then resell - so it seems like a battery recycling method, but not one that would improve the lifespan of a battery once it has been given to a user. There also doesn't seem like there is a safe way for someone to do this on their own, so this would be incoorperated on the coorperation side for recycling batteries (which there is already a collection and recycling process attempted).
