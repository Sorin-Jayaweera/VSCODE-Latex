
If a photon hits a solar panel with more than 1 band gap worth of energy, that is usually lost. By catenating multiple materials together multiple band gap transitions can increase the efficiency of solar panels by generating multiple excitons per each photon with excess energy. This was explored using quantum dot rods with tunable band gap (three gaps were explored). The energy efficiency of the multiple exciton doesn't scale linearly in the way we would expect - a three band gap energy photon generates twice the amount of power. However, this is still a significant improvement in solar panel efficiency.
