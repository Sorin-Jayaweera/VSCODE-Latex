- Describe how the system works overall
Air (mainly oxygen and CO2 are useful) is pumped into a chamber with a porous ceramic MOF. Sunlight is collected from a wide area and focused onto the ceramic, which heats up the block. In some way this works as catalysis to make a drop in fuel (of similar efficiencies of liquid fuels, such as kerosene or gasoline, etc). They produce a unique liquid with different ratios depending on the end goal, with similar levels of contamination from aerosols as are in useful fuels. 

- Describe in-depth how one element works
The Direct Air capture is (to me) the most interesting step. A fan pulls ambient air through a filter which absorbs all ambient water for extraction, after which there are CO2 filters. A vacuum pump aerolizes materials (i think), then a condensor extracts water ( which doesn't aeralize in the way that CO2 does?) and passes the CO2 to a compressor for highd ensity CO2/

- Why are temperature & pressure cycling important?
The temperature and pressure control the oxygen exchange with ceria (the ceramic), which changes the relative proportions put together downstream.
