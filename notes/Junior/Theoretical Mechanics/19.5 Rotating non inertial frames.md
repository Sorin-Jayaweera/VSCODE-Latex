
We want to work in principle axis bases, because its fairly nice to represent angular moment and angular velocity.

$$
\begin{align}
\vec{l}=\mathcal{I}\vec{\omega}=\lambda \vec{\omega}
\end{align}
$$

When rotation occurs, its a linear combination of principle axes
$$
\begin{align}
\vec{l} = \lambda_{1}\omega_{x}'+ \lambda_{2}\omega'_{y} + \lambda_{3}\omega_{z}'
\end{align}
$$

In a rotating rame, we have pseudoforces.
