If we have a function with eigenvalues given by
$$
\begin{align}
\hat{H} \ket{E} = E \ket{E}  
\end{align}
$$
And a generator using that
$$
\begin{align}
\hat{U}(t)\ket{E}  & = e^{\frac{-i}{\hbar}\hat{H}t}\ket{E}   \\
 & = e^{\frac{-i}{\hbar}Et}\ket{E} 
\end{align}
$$
We just throw in the eigenvalue because the $\hat{H}$ always applies to $\ket{E}$ in the Taylor expansion.
