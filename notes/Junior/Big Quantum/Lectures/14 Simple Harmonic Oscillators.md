This is the primary potential that quantizing electromagnetism etc outputs. Phonons, waves, optics, anything of that nature. 

$$
\begin{align}
\hat{H}_{\text{ sho }} = \hbar \omega\left( \hat{N}+\frac{1}{2} \right) \\
\text{ where } \\
\hat{N} \equiv  \hat{a}^{\dagger}\hat{a}
\end{align}
$$

Where $\hat{N}\ket{\eta }=\eta \ket{\eta}$

$$
\begin{align}
\eta  & \geq 0 \\
\hat{a} \ket{\eta} &  = c_{\eta} \ket{\eta-1}  \\
\hat{a}^{\dagger}\ket{\eta} &  = \hat{c}_{+\eta} \ket{eta} \\
\eta  & = n \in 0,1,2,3 = \mathbb{N} \\
E_{n} &  = \hbar \omega\left( n+\frac{1}{2} \right) \\
\hat{a}^{\dagger} \ket{n}  & = \sqrt[]{ n+1 } \ket{n+1}  \\
\hat{a}\ket{n}  & = \sqrt[]{ n } \ket{n-1}  
\end{align}
$$
