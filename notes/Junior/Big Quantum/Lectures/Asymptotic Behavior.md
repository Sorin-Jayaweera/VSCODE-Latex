The radial equation
$$
\begin{align}
\left[ -\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2\mu}\left( \frac{ d ^{2}}{d r^{2} } + \frac{2}{r}\frac{ d }{d r } \right)  \frac{l(l+1 )}{2\mu r^{2}}\right] R(r) = E R(r)
\end{align}
$$
And
$$
\begin{align}
R(r) = \frac{u(r)}{r}
\end{align}
$$
Show that
$$
\begin{align}
\left[ -\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2\mu}\frac{ d ^{2}}{d r^{2} } + \frac{l(l+1)}{2\mu r^{2}}+ V(r) \right]u(r) = E u(r)
\end{align}
$$

My answer:
This looks like
$$
\begin{align}
\left[ -\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2\mu}\frac{ d ^{2}}{d r^{2} } + \frac{l(l+1)}{2\mu r^{2}}+ V(r) \right] \frac{u(r)}{r} = E \frac{u(r)}{r} + \left( \frac{\hbar^{2}}{2\mu} \frac{2}{r}\frac{ d }{d r }  \right)\frac{u(r)}{r} \\
\left[ -\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2\mu}\frac{ d ^{2}}{d r^{2} } + \frac{l(l+1)}{2\mu r^{2}}+ V(r) \right] u(r) = E u(r) + r\left( \frac{\hbar^{2}}{2\mu} \frac{2}{r}\frac{ d }{d r }  \right)\frac{u(r)}{r}
\end{align}
$$
Now we simplify 
$$
\begin{align}
Eu(r)  & \stackrel{?}{=}   E u(r) + r\left( \frac{\hbar^{2}}{2\mu} \frac{2}{r} \frac{ d }{dr} \frac{u(r)}{r}\right)  \\
 & = E u(r) + \left(  \frac{\hbar^{2}}{\mu} \frac{u'(r)}{r}- \frac{u(r)}{r^{2}} \right)
\end{align}
$$
