## Bell's Inequaltiy
There are physical situations where you can distinguish between a classical source spitting out HH or VV and a quantum thing which has them entangled. We don't have Newtons flaming laser sword. If we don't look at nice angles - i.e. 0. 90. or 45 degrees - then we have cool stuff happening.

The game:
two players go to opposite sides of a casino. A dealer will flip a coin with each person, and they write down an $X$ or an $O$. The players win if they wrote the same thing down and the coins were in Heads Heads or opposite. If the coins are in tails tails, then they win if they have opposite marks. Bells inequality says that the best you can ever do is win $\frac{3}{4}$ of the times (it is provable that just writing down xx every time is just the best). 

There are two physicists that win 85% of the time. How? 
They make a pair of entangled photons and store them (store in fiber optic cable or an atom state). The dealer flips a coin.

Alice rotates the photon 45 degrees if it is tails. 
Bob turns the photon 22.5 degrees left or right (heads vs tails).

If the photon passes, then they write O. If it is absorbed/reflected, they write an X.

The angle between the polarizers is always 22.5 degrees apart for the heads heads or heads tails, but in the tails tails (45 degrees and -22.5) then they are 67.5 degrees apart.
