## Momentum Space

Momentum eigenstates
$$
\begin{align}
\hat{p}_{x} \ket{p} = p\ket{p}  
\end{align}
$$
Wavefunction in momentum
$$
\begin{align}
\braket{ p | \psi } 
\end{align}
$$
$$
\begin{align}
\bra{x} \hat{p}_{x} \ket{p} = p \braket{ x | p } = \frac{\hbar}{i}\frac{ \partial  }{ \partial x } \braket{ x | p } 
\end{align}
$$

$$
\begin{align}
\ket{p} = \int_{}^{} dx \ket{x} \braket{ x | p }  
\end{align}
$$

Debroglie wavelength
$$
\begin{align}
p \frac{\lambda}{h}= 2\pi \\
\lambda= \frac{h}{p}
\end{align}
$$
$$
\begin{align}
\braket{ p | \psi } = \int dx \, \frac{1}{\sqrt[]{ 2\pi \hbar } }e^{\frac{-ipx}{\hbar}} \braket{ x | \psi } \\
\braket{ x | \psi } = \int dp \, \frac{1}{\sqrt[]{ 2\pi \hbar } }e^{\frac{ipx}{\hbar}}\braket{ p | \psi }  
\end{align}
$$
