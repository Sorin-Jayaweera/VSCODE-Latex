## 9.1
three dimensional operators are just the products of the one dimensional case, since each direction is orthogonal. Instead of single integrals we have triple, and bras/kets are now expressed as
$$
\begin{align}
\braket{ x,y,z | \psi }  
\end{align}
$$
We can express things radially, and with gradients now. 

## 9.2

We have
$$
\begin{align}
\mathbf{\hat{p}}_{1}^{2} = \hat{p}_{1x}^{2} + \hat{p}_{1y}^{2} + \hat{p}_{1z}^{2}   
\end{align}
$$

Inversion is a discrete symmetry (parity operator)
