## 7.1
SHO arise from Taylor expanding functions very close to equilibrium. Derivative is zero, and potential matters relative, so we can ignore offsets - that gives just the 
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{1}{2}kx^{2} 
\end{align}
$$
potential term. Everything is a Simple Harmonic Oscillator!
