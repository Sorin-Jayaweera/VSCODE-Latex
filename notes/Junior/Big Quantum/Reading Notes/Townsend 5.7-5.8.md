## 5.7



The density operator tells us the particle in a region of phase space instead of a pure single state - since we can't perfectly separate out particles in real experiment.

For a pure state
$$
\begin{align}
\hat{\rho} = \ket{\psi} \bra{\psi} 
\end{align}
$$
which is the identity. Non pure mixed states have $\hat{\rho}^{2}\leq 1$.
