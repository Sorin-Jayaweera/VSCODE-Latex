We have the general form for a Michelson interferometer
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{I_{\text{ out }}}{I_{\text{ in }} } = \frac{\left< \left( \frac{1}{2}\left| E(t)-E(t+\tau) \right| \right)^{2}  \right> }{\left< \left| E(t) \right| ^{2} \right> } 
\end{align}
$$
and
$$
\begin{align}
g^{(1)}(\tau) = \int_{0}^{\infty} \cos \omega \tau s(\omega)d\omega 
\end{align}
$$
