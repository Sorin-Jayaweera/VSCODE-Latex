## The concept of Anxiety

>[!bug] my thoughts
>Is Kierkergaard's anxiety really necessary for self awareness? Does it 
camus uses absurdism to dispel anxiety and cope with it

Anxiety is the awareness of freedom, the possibility of being able to. This anxiety is self-driven and (largely) aimless, not from external factors - there is no worry of good or evil or interactions that are forced / driven by others. It is core to the human experience, stuck between beast and angel. Freedom is infinite, and one can never fully exercise or explore the whole space of things available to them. We are weighted down by the reality that we have to make choices and actions in the world. Freedom is an expensive burden, the anxiety of choice prevents us from innocence and nothingness. We are something. In this way, self consciousness is a disease that we can't get rid of.
