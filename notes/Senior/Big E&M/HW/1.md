We can replace $d\tau= \frac{dt}{\gamma}$
$$
\begin{align}
\gamma\frac{ d p_{\mu} }{d t }  & = \frac{q}{c} u^{\nu}F_{\mu \nu} \\
 & = \frac{q}{c} (u^{0}F_{\mu,0} + u^{i}F_{\mu,i}  )
\end{align}
$$
Because $F_{\mu,\nu} = -F_{\nu,\mu}$, we have 

$$
\begin{align}
\gamma \frac{ d p_{\mu} }{d t } & = -\frac{q}{c} (u^{0}F_{0,\mu} + u^{i}F_{i,\mu}  )
\end{align}
$$

We know that $u^{0}F_{0,\mu} = -cF_{\mu}$, and we can relabel $F_{\mu}=\vec{E}$. This gives us

$$
\begin{align}
\gamma \frac{dp_{\mu}}{dt} = q \vec{E} - \frac{q}{c} u^{i}F_{\mu i} 
\end{align}
$$
